The challenge in primary care

The earliest window. The biggest constraint.

Primary-care physicians are the front line for catching MCI — but a 12-minute Annual Wellness Visit isn't enough room to administer a paper screener, score it, and counsel the patient on what comes next. Home Memory Screen removes that friction.

Why offer it

Do a workup. Don't “wait and see.”

Working up patients who are aging normally wastes clinical resources, neuropsych slots, and patient confidence. Delaying the workup in patients whose cognition is genuinely declining costs them the window where intervention works.

The Home Memory Screen lets you do the workup only on patients who need one. 97% specificity means very few false positives. 95% sensitivity means very few cases missed. Built for primary care.

What your clinic gets

  • Billable workflow. Reimbursable under CPT 99483, G0136, and G0444.
  • No specialist credentials. Staff-administered. No training certification required.
  • Under 10 minutes. Fits into the existing Annual Wellness Visit workflow.
  • Clinician-ready report. Plain language for the patient, clinical sub-scores for you.
  • Optional patient portal. Patients can take the screen at home before their visit.
  • Audit-ready documentation. Helps defend HCC coding through RADV.
